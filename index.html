Coaching Team

Driver Development
Team

Coaches who focus on racecraft, discipline, and preparing drivers for competitive racing.

Head Coach · English-Speaking Program

David Guibal

David Guibal is a professional karting coach and the original Founder of Infinity Racing in Vietnam. Coming from a racing family, with his father involved in Citroën Racing during the Sébastien Loeb era, David brings deep motorsport experience from competing across Asia in the Senior Rotax Series. He helps drivers develop racecraft, kart control, setup understanding, and competitive confidence from beginner to advanced levels.

Simulator Coach

Blayke Philips

Blayke Philips is a simulator coach specializing in remote driver development and race preparation. He helps drivers improve consistency, decision-making, racing lines, braking technique, and race scenarios through structured simulator training. His coaching supports on-track progress by helping drivers build confidence, discipline, and sharper racecraft between track sessions.

Head Coach · Vietnamese-Speaking Program

Steven Nguyen

Nguyen Trung Quan (Steven Nguyen) is a professional motorsport instructor and Co-Founder of Speed Master Club. He has trained multiple Pro Kart programs, helping drivers develop racing technique, vehicle control, safety awareness, and confidence from beginner to advanced levels.
